# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully regardless of experience, identity or background. Give specific, constructive feedback; debate ideas and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in repository issues, PRs and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeated/severe offenders. They should explain moderation decisions privately where feasible and consider context consistently. Report concerns through the maintainer's GitHub profile contact options, requesting a private channel before sharing sensitive details. Do not post personal information publicly. If the concern involves a maintainer, use GitHub's abuse-reporting mechanisms. Maintainers protect reporter privacy as far as practical and prohibit retaliation.
